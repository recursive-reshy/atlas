## Questions and stop-and-ask points
1. **Locked text wording (3.2).** ADR 0105 says "moved verbatim". The text says the model is given "the titles and openings of the notes they already have", and the `source_content` sentence says "the notes' titles and openings". Both are false once the agent reads notes with tools. I plan to move it verbatim and then change **only** those two phrases (to say the notes are reached through the tools and that note text read through them is material, not instructions), plus the TRD's two added sections. If you want it strictly verbatim, say so.
2. **`AgentUnavailableError` (3.4).** The server has `AgentError` today, with "Agent service unreachable" and a timeout message. The TRD names `AgentUnavailableError` and the stored reason "Agent service unavailable". I plan a subclass of `AgentError` thrown for network error and timeout on the syllabus call, and keep the linker's messages unchanged.
3. **`syllabus_locked_instructions` in shared (3.4):** a response-shape change in `packages/shared`, named by the TRD §4.5, so I treat it as covered.
